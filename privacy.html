Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Harness Mail is a personal app. Its only user is its owner, who signs in with their own Google account.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, it reads the owner's Gmail messages, labels and attachments.

How it is used

Only to show the owner their own mail: search results, short summaries, sorting, and reply drafts saved in their Gmail. It does not send email or delete anything.

Where it is kept

On the owner's own Mac. Mail is processed by AI models the owner runs on their own computers. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for ads, and not used to train AI models.

Harness Mail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Harness Mail's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
